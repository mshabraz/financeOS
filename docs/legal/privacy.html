FinanceOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

FinanceOS is a personal, self-hosted finance application. It is not operated as a commercial public cloud service.

Data storage

Your financial data is stored only on the device or server where you run FinanceOS. The application operator does not maintain a central database of your transactions.

Bank connections

When you connect Swedbank, SEB, or other supported banks, account information is accessed through Enable Banking (a PSD2 open banking provider) only after you give explicit consent at your bank. Imported transactions and balances are stored locally in your FinanceOS database.

Data sharing

Your data is not sold. It is not shared with third parties except:

  • Enable Banking and your bank, as required to fetch account information you request;
  • Market data providers (e.g. exchange rates, investment prices) when you use those features.

Data retention

You control how long data is kept. You may delete your FinanceOS database or individual imports at any time on your own server.
